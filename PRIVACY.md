# Privacy policy

_Last updated: November 6, 2026_

Plainshot is a browser extension that saves a screenshot of the current web
page to your computer.

## What Plainshot collects

Nothing. Plainshot does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any
personal data or browsing data.

## How it works with your data

- Plainshot only reads the tab you explicitly invoke it on, by clicking its
  toolbar button or pressing its keyboard shortcut. It has no access to any
  other tab or site.
- The screenshot is created inside your browser and written directly to your
  downloads folder. It never leaves your computer.
- Plainshot makes no network requests. It contains no analytics, no
  telemetry, no advertising and no remote code.
- Plainshot stores no settings and keeps no history of what you captured.

## Report a problem

The "Report a problem" entry in the toolbar button's menu opens a GitHub
issue form in a new tab, pre-filled with the Plainshot version, browser name
and version, operating system, interface language and the current time. The
address and content of the page you were on are not included. Nothing is
sent unless you choose to submit that form on GitHub, in which case GitHub's
own privacy policy applies.
